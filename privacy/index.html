Privacy

Short version: this site collects as little as possible, and nothing happens behind your back.

The join form

If you fill in the form on the Join page, what you write (name, email, optional phone or Instagram, company name, your message) is delivered to the Sapiens team’s private Telegram chat. We use it only to reply to you. We don’t add you to any lists, and we don’t share it with anyone.

Cookies & tracking

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Your browser stores exactly one thing locally: your language choice (EN/CZ), which never leaves your device.

External services

Fonts, scripts and images are all served from this website itself — visiting doesn’t contact Google or any ad network. When you submit the join form, your message travels through Telegram’s Bot API to reach us.

Your data, your call

Want something you sent us deleted? Message us on Instagram and it’s gone.
